# Pointer: 30-second demo video script

One homeowner, one problem, the hand working, the readiness report. About 75 words of narration,
read at a relaxed pace. The homeowner is **made up**; say so on screen.

| Time | On screen | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | Title card: a made-up Anaheim homeowner, a backyard, a stack of city forms. Caption: "Made-up homeowner. Real city checklist." | "Rosa wants to add a small home in her backyard. Every missing item on the city's checklist sends her application back." |
| 0:05–0:10 | Pointer opens. Anaheim's real checklist PDF on the left, Rosa's draft on the right. The fast-path card reads "You may qualify for ADU Express", with the source link and date checked. | "Pointer finds her fastest path first." |
| 0:10–0:20 | The hand glides to a checklist item and it turns green. The next item turns red: "Site plan not found". Law Lens glows on "Gov Code 65852.2" and shows "now 66310–66342" with an HCD source chip. The action log scrolls beside it. | "Then it walks the city's own checklist, one item at a time. It catches what's missing, and it catches the city citing a law that was renumbered in 2024." |
| 0:20–0:27 | Readiness report: complete, missing, questions to ask the city. Brief cut to the before-and-after redaction panel. | "Rosa leaves with a list: what's done, what's missing, what to ask. Her personal details never left her laptop." |
| 0:27–0:30 | End card: "Pointer. Anaheim live today; 33 more cities are one file each." Footer: "Prototype. Not legal advice." | "Pointer. Get it right the first time." |

## Recording notes

- Record from the replay file (`fixtures/replay.json`) so the take doesn't depend on wifi or the API.
- Keep the "not legal advice" banner and the source chips in frame; judges look for them.
- "Rosa" and her documents are made up. Use only the made-up applicant fixtures.
- If ADU Express eligibility hasn't been confirmed from the city page by recording time, change
  the card to "Fast path: unknown — confirm with the city" and drop the 0:05 line.
- Don't add numbers to the narration (pages, weeks, dollars) unless they come from a cited source.
